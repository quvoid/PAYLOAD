The five rules that hold it together

  1. Every product sits in exactly one category. That category is its home, and it's what generates the breadcrumb.
  2. Nothing is more than 3 clicks from the homepage. Home → silo → category → product. That's the deepest it goes.
  3. Products link sideways only inside their own silo. A whey protein suggests other whey proteins — never a sunscreen.
  4. Cross-silo links go through brand and topic hubs only. Those pages are the bridges; nothing else crosses.
  5. Editors never type a link. Every link is built from the product's category, brand and aspects — so it can't be forgotten, broken, or inconsistent.
